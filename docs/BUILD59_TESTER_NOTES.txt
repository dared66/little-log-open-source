What’s new
• Switch nursing sides directly from the running timer, including on the Lock Screen.
• More efficient syncing when your log has lots of entries.
• Back up an existing log and share a caregiver invitation from Settings.
• Simpler setup and more consistent layouts.

What to try
• With another caregiver, add or edit an entry and check that it appears while both apps are open.
• Log an entry offline, reopen the app, then reconnect and check it reaches the shared log.

• Share an invitation with another caregiver and check that it opens the correct log.
